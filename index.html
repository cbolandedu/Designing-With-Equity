Equity Framework Explorer

A shared framework for equitable, student-centered teaching, built from the BETTER in STEM project and now used across disciplines. It has four components. Choose one to explore its indicators, see what they look like in different fields, and jot your own reflections.

How to use this site

Each component has a vision and a set of indicators. Open any indicator to see why it matters, small changes to try, examples from across disciplines, and reflection questions. Use the "My notes" tab to capture your thinking. Your notes stay private in this browser, and you can copy them into Canvas anytime.
