The vision

Culture & Environment is about helping every student feel they belong, in our classrooms and in our fields, and making sure they know what it takes to succeed.

Belonging grows when students believe the ideas, experiences, and resources they bring are recognized and put to use, and when they have a voice and agency in their own learning. It also means students see people of many backgrounds contributing to their field, understand how the dominant culture has shaped what their field values, and explore multiple cultural ways of knowing.
